## A movement for change

The Young Women’s Christian Association (YWCA) is a global movement working for the empowerment, leadership and rights of women, young women and girls in more than 120 countries. Our members and supporters come from different faiths, ages, backgrounds, beliefs and cultures, connected by a shared commitment to dignity, equality and justice.

In India, the YWCA has been a pioneering force in the women’s rights movement since 1896. Through its 94 local associations, the YWCA of India works across communities to challenge gender-based inequality and create opportunities for people who are too often left behind.

> We believe that lasting change begins when people have the power, knowledge and opportunity to shape their own lives.
